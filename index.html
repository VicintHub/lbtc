OUR CONVICTION

Talent is Distributed Across Nigeria. Opportunity is Not.

Every academic session, thousands of high-achieving Nigerian youths clear their WAEC and JAMB examinations with distinction, only to watch their dreams slip away because their families cannot afford tertiary tuition, registration fees, or basic living expenses.

Let's Build The Child Foundation was born out of a profound conviction by Sir Itiako and Lady Peikumor Ikpokpo, KSM: that a nation's true strength lies in how intentionally it invests in its most promising children.

HOLISTIC METHODOLOGY

Three Pillars of Child Development

True empowerment requires more than financial relief. Our model integrates academics with character and practical life skills.

01

Character Development

Values & Leadership

Instilling unyielding integrity, self-discipline, compassionate leadership, and a deep commitment to community service. We build individuals who hold themselves accountable to the highest ethical standards in public and private life.

02

Academic Excellence

Scholarships & Mentorship

Providing undergraduate scholarships, learning resources, and executive mentorship to high-performing students enrolled in accredited Nigerian public tertiary institutions (universities and polytechnics).

03

Skills Acquisition

Self-Reliance & Enterprise

Equipping scholars with practical entrepreneurial, technological, and life skills. Our practical workshops ensure beneficiaries graduate not only with degrees, but with the competence to thrive independently.

SCHOLARSHIP PATHWAYS

Empowering Educational Journeys

Three distinct pathways designed to support Nigerian tertiary students from admission through graduation.

Freshers Scholarship

For newly admitted 100L/ND1 students in public tertiary institutions needing support to commence studies.

Status: Open Cycle View Freshers Scholarship Details

Returning Students Renewal

For continuing undergraduate scholars maintaining CGPA >= 3.50 and good conduct.

Status: Annual Renewal View Returning Students Renewal Details

UTME Financial Aid

JAMB registration fee support for promising secondary school graduates facing financial hardship.
