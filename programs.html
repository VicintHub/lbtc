EDUCATIONAL PATHWAYS

Scholarships & Support Programmes

Three structured pathways helping ambitious Nigerian students access, continue, and complete their tertiary education.

PATHWAY 1 • NEWLY ADMITTED

Freshers Scholarship

Current Cycle Deadline: Sept 30th

For candidates who have recently secured admission into an accredited public Nigerian university or polytechnic and need financial support to begin their academic journey without delay.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Admitted into a public tertiary institution (University or Polytechnic) in Nigeria.
  • Verified financial need & community attestation.
  • Minimum 5 credits in WAEC/NECO (including Maths & English) in one sitting.
  • Age between 16 and 25 years.
  • Commitment to leadership & character development workshops.

Required Documents

  • Tertiary Institution Admission Letter
  • Official JAMB Result Slip
  • O'Level WAEC/NECO Result (1 Sitting)
  • Birth Certificate / Age Declaration
  • Local Government Area (LGA) Identification
  • Two Recommendation Letters (Principal / Community Leader)
Apply Now for Freshers Scholarship
PATHWAY 2 • CONTINUING STUDENTS

Returning Students Renewal

Annual Renewal Window

For continuing undergraduate students who have completed at least one academic session and wish to sustain their scholarship by maintaining academic excellence.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Enrolled full-time in an accredited public tertiary institution.
  • Completed at least one (1) full academic session.
  • Minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 3.50 / 5.00.
  • Clean disciplinary record & exemplary conduct.

Conditions for Renewal

  • Maintain the required minimum 3.50 CGPA.
  • Pass all registered course units each semester.
  • Prompt submission of official academic transcripts.
  • Active participation in Foundation mentorship engagements.
Apply / Renew Returning Scholarship
PATHWAY 3 • ENTRANCE EXAM AID

UTME Financial Aid

Registration Grant Window

Direct financial relief for promising secondary school graduates preparing for UTME / JAMB matriculation examinations who face fee registration roadblocks.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Secondary school graduate with strong academic standing.
  • Verified financial constraint preventing JAMB fee payment.
  • Attestation letter from Secondary School Principal.

Required Entry Documents

  • O'Level WAEC/NECO Statement of Result
  • Birth Certificate or LGA Identification
  • Principal Recommendation Letter

TRANSPARENT WORKFLOW

How We Review & Award Scholarships

Our 4-stage selection workflow ensures complete integrity, objectivity, and merit-driven awarding.

1

Application Submission

Submit complete application form and credentials before deadline.

2

Document Verification

Verification of WAEC/NECO/JAMB results and financial need attestations.

3

Shortlist & Interview

Shortlisted candidates participate in a panel interview evaluating character and vision.

4

Award & Mentorship

Scholars receive tuition funding and are paired with executive mentors.
