OUR ORIGIN & PHILOSOPHY

Laying the Foundation for a Prosperous Tomorrow

LET'S BUILD THE CHILD FOUNDATION was established to address a persistent, heartbreaking reality across Nigeria: brilliant, hardworking children who possess academic potential, strong character, and a genuine desire to serve humanity, yet are constrained by severe economic hardship.

We believe that every child with talent and determination deserves the chance to thrive. Our focus is on identifying these promising young individuals early, providing them with full financial support, leadership mentorship, and character formation so they can emerge as ethical leaders and agents of positive societal change.

The ₦10,000,000.00 Annual Endowment

To advance this initiative with institutional strength, the Foundation has committed a Ten Million Naira annual endowment for its first five years, providing guaranteed, sustainable support for all program beneficiaries.

OUR VISION

To Build a Future Where No Gifted Child is Denied Growth

To raise healthy, educated, empowered, and responsible children by providing equal opportunities for growth regardless of their socioeconomic background—laying the foundation for stronger families, healthier communities, and a prosperous nation.

OUR MISSION

Holistic Empowerment Through Education & Values

To improve the lives of children through character development, education, healthcare, life skills acquisition, empowerment, and opportunity creation across public tertiary institutions and local communities in Nigeria.
