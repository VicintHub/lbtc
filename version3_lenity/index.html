OUR STORY & CONVICTION

Building Leaders, Transforming Families

Let's Build The Child Foundation was established to address a systemic challenge across Nigeria: brilliant students clearing WAEC and JAMB, yet unable to afford university fees.

Our focus is on identifying bright, hardworking children who demonstrate academic potential, strong character, and a genuine commitment to serving humanity.
